All six final assets were produced with the built-in image generation workflow. The matching ET official photographs in `../et-official-reference/` were used as identity references. The official light-studio photographs remain unchanged and are not used as the final dark-metal gallery or panel background.

Scene constraints: natural approximately 2.4:1 horizontal extension; deep black and charcoal metal studio; subtle steel-panel and dark-brick layers; restrained vertical red light strips; wet micro-reflective floor; thin haze; consistent camera height and perspective. The panel adds localized red smoke and rim light without tinting the full frame.

Acceptance constraints: each image must show one complete ET with safe margin and no duplicate angle, cross-model content, embedded black bar/column, artificial letterboxing, or cropped wheel, handlebar, fender, or tail. CSS cropping, `object-position`, masks, or background colors must not be used to hide an asset defect.
